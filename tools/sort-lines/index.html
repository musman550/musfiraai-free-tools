Sort Lines

Published 2026-11-04

Sort Lines sorts text from A to Z or Z to A in your browser for free.

Sort Lines - What it does

The Sort Lines tool organizes lines alphabetically from A to Z or in reverse order from Z to A. It offers options to ignore case sensitivity, trim leading and trailing spaces, and skip empty lines during sorting. Additionally, it removes any duplicate lines while performing the sort. As you type or paste text into the tool, the sorted result updates in real-time. This browser-based utility does not upload or store your text data, ensuring privacy. It handles typical tasks such as organizing a list of names, arranging keywords, and preparing data for spreadsheets. Note that sorting may be slower on older devices when dealing with very long lists.

Sort Lines - Limits & Testing

The Sort Lines tool was rigorously tested in real browsers at various screen sizes—both desktop and mobile—with sample inputs to ensure accurate sorting from A to Z or from Z to A. Results were checked against expected values to confirm reliability. The tool can ignore case, trim spaces, skip empty lines, and remove duplicates while sorting. It processes text entirely within the browser, ensuring no data is uploaded or stored. Known limits include plain character order for sorting (accented letters follow z), numbers being sorted as text (10 comes before 9), and potential slowness on older phones with very long lists.

Sort Lines - Tips for Best Results

To get the best results from the Sort Lines tool, ensure your text is clean and relevant to your needs. Start by trimming spaces and ignoring case sensitivity if necessary. This helps in accurately sorting your lines. You can also remove any duplicate entries as you type, which keeps your list neat and reduces clutter. Remember that the tool sorts based on plain character order, so accented letters will sort after 'z', and numbers are sorted alphabetically (e.g., "10" before "9"). The tool runs locally in your browser, so no text is uploaded or stored. This makes it ideal for quick tasks like tidying a list of names, organizing keywords, cleaning data before comparison, or preparing entries for a spreadsheet.

Sort Lines - Questions & Answers

Does Sort Lines upload my text?

No, your text is processed only in your browser and never uploaded or stored.

Can I sort by custom order?

Sort Lines currently sorts alphabetically from A to Z or Z to A only.

How do I remove duplicates?

After sorting, use the ‘Remove Repeats’ feature to eliminate duplicate lines.

Is Sort Lines case-sensitive?

Sort Lines can ignore case when sorting and trimming spaces.

Does it work with special characters?

Sort Lines sorts based on alphabetical order, including special characters.
