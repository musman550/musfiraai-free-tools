Word Frequency Counter: What It Does

The Word Frequency Counter tool counts how often each word appears in your text and sorts them by frequency, allowing you to easily identify the most common words. It can be configured to ignore common filler words and set a minimum word length, while treating uppercase and lowercase letters as the same. The tool operates entirely within your browser, ensuring that no text is uploaded or stored. It helps in finding overused words in essays, identifying main topics of a document, performing keyword checks, and classroom exercises. Note that it only counts words made up of the letters a to z, digits, and apostrophes, and its performance might be slower on older phones due to the length of the text.

Word Frequency Counter: Testing & Limits

The tool was rigorously tested by inputting sample texts into real browsers at both desktop and phone screen sizes. The results were then cross-checked against expected values to ensure accuracy. However, the tool has known limitations: it only counts words made of letters a to z, digits, and apostrophes, so other alphabets are not supported. It uses a filler word list that is specific to English. Additionally, very long texts may be slow to process on older phones. Despite these limits, the tool is useful for finding overused words in essays, identifying main topics in texts, performing keyword checks, and classroom exercises.

Word Frequency Counter: Get Best Results

To get the best results from the Word Frequency Counter, ensure your text includes only letters, digits, and apostrophes, as support for other alphabets is not available. Use the tool to ignore common filler words, set a minimum word length to focus on meaningful terms, and remember that it treats uppercase and lowercase versions of the same word as identical.
