Loan EMI Calculator

Published 2026-10-07

The Loan EMI Calculator works out your monthly loan payment, total interest and year by year balance, and shows what an extra payment saves. It is free, runs in your browser and needs no signup.

What the Loan EMI Calculator shows you

Enter the loan amount, the yearly interest rate and the term in years or months, and the monthly payment appears at once. Next to it you see the total interest, the total payment and the number of payments needed, plus a year by year table of principal paid, interest paid and the balance still owed. An extra monthly payment field shows how many months and how much interest you could save by paying a little more. Numbers can be shown in the international style or in the lakh and crore style used in South Asia, and the tool works with any currency because it only does arithmetic.

How we checked the Loan EMI Calculator and where it stops

We compared the monthly payment, total interest and number of payments with an independent calculation made with high precision decimal arithmetic, using many combinations of amount, rate and term. We also ran the screen tests on this page on a desktop and a phone screen. The tool uses the standard reducing balance formula with monthly compounding. Results are estimates: a lender may add fees or insurance, may round differently, and may use a floating rate that changes over time. Payment dates are not modeled, so the schedule assumes equal monthly periods.

Getting useful answers from the Loan EMI Calculator

Compare offers by keeping the amount the same and changing only the rate or the term. A longer term lowers the monthly payment, but it usually raises the total interest, so look at both numbers. Try an extra monthly payment even if it is small, because it goes straight to the loan balance and shortens the schedule. Before you rely on a result, ask the lender for the full repayment schedule and compare the first rows. Your figures stay in the browser and are not stored, so you can test different situations freely.

  • Checking the effect of paying extra each month.

Loan EMI Calculator questions and answers

Is the Loan EMI Calculator free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no signup and has no limit on how many loans you can try.

Does the Loan EMI Calculator store my loan details?

No. The numbers are used only in your browser to do the arithmetic. They are not uploaded or saved, and they disappear when you close the page.

Which formula does the Loan EMI Calculator use?

It uses the standard reducing balance formula with monthly compounding. If the rate is 0, the loan is simply divided evenly across the payments.

Why is my bank's EMI different from the Loan EMI Calculator?

Banks may add fees or insurance, round differently, or use a changing rate. Use this result as an estimate and ask your lender for the lender's own schedule.

Can the Loan EMI Calculator work with my currency?

Yes. It does not convert money, it only calculates, so you can enter any currency. Choose the number style that matches how you write amounts.
