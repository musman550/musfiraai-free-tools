What Remove Extra Spaces Does

The Remove Extra Spaces tool collapses multiple spaces into single ones and trims spaces and tabs from the ends of each line while keeping your original line breaks intact. It can also remove blank lines and convert tabs to spaces, all without uploading or storing your text. This tool is particularly useful for cleaning up text copied from PDFs, tidying email drafts, preparing data for spreadsheets, and fixing code comments. For instance, it can handle the extra spaces in a long document copied from a PDF efficiently, ensuring that each line starts and ends with clean, readable text. Note that while it only treats spaces and tabs as extra spaces, other invisible characters are preserved. Additionally, very long texts might be slow to process on older devices.
