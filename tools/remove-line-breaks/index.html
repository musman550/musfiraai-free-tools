Remove Line Breaks joins text into one line with options, free and no signup.

Remove Line Breaks: What It Does

This tool joins lines into one by removing line breaks, allowing you to choose whether to replace them with a space, a comma, or nothing at all. It keeps paragraph breaks intact and trims each line while skipping empty ones. The process runs entirely in your browser, ensuring that no text is uploaded or stored elsewhere. Recognizing paragraphs through blank lines only, it’s ideal for cleaning text copied from PDFs, preparing single-line values, creating comma-separated lists, and fixing broken paragraphs. However, be aware that poems and similar texts may require manual adjustments due to their reliance on line breaks for meaning. Very long texts might take longer to process on older phones.
