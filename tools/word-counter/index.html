Word Counter

Published 2026-10-06

The Word Counter counts words, characters, sentences and reading time as you type, and shows how close your text is to common limits. It is free, runs in your browser and needs no signup.

What the Word Counter shows while you type

Paste or type into the box and the numbers update at once. You see words, characters with and without spaces, sentences and paragraphs, plus a reading time based on 238 words per minute and a speaking time based on 150 words per minute. Four meters show how close your text is to common limits: 280 characters for a short social post, 160 for a search result description, 160 for a text message and 2,200 for a photo caption. A list of the eight most used words, with filler words left out, helps you spot repeated wording.

How we checked the Word Counter and where it stops

Before publishing, we ran the tool with a real sample sentence on a desktop screen and on a phone screen, and compared each number with the expected result. The screenshots on this page come from those runs. The counter has limits you should know about. For languages written without spaces, word counts depend on the segmenter built into your browser. The meters count characters rather than bytes, and a platform may count emoji or links differently. Reading and speaking times are averages, not a prediction for any one person.

Getting cleaner results from the Word Counter

Write first and count at the end, so the numbers do not interrupt your thinking. When you are close to a limit, look at the most used words and trim repeated phrases before cutting useful content. For a search description, write one clear sentence that fits inside the meter, then read it aloud once. For a talk, divide the speaking time by the number of slides to see how long each one gets. Nothing typed here is uploaded or stored, and the clear button removes your text from the page.

Word Counter questions answered quickly

Is the Word Counter free to use?

Yes. It is free, needs no signup and has no usage limit on the page, so you can paste text as often as you like.

Does the Word Counter store or upload my text?

No. All counting happens in your browser. The text is not sent anywhere or saved, and it disappears when you clear the box or close the tab.

How does the Word Counter work out reading time?

It divides the word count by 238 words per minute for reading and by 150 words per minute for speaking. Real speeds vary, so treat the result as a rough guide.

Can the Word Counter handle other languages?

It works with many languages. For writing systems without spaces it relies on your browser's word segmenter, so counts can differ slightly from other tools.

Why does another site show a different number than the Word Counter?

Tools disagree about hyphens, numbers, links and emoji. Check the rule used where you will submit your text, and trust that count for strict limits.
