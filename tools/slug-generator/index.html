Slug Generator: Create Slugs

The Slug Generator turns your title into a lowercase slug by removing accents and converting any letters to the a-z range. You can choose between hyphens or underscores as separators, and it collapses repeated symbols and spaces into one separator while trimming the ends. This tool operates entirely in your browser, ensuring that no text is uploaded or stored. It supports typical uses such as creating blog post addresses, naming files, building product URLs, and tidying a list of titles. Note that letters outside the a-z range are dropped, common words aren't removed, and the result might require manual checking if the title is mostly symbols.

Slug Generator: Browser-Based Tool

The Slug Generator was tested by running it in real browsers at both desktop and phone screen sizes using various sample inputs. The results were checked against expected values to ensure accuracy. Known limits include removing accents from letters so "café" becomes "cafe," allowing the choice between hyphens or underscores as separators, collapsing repeated symbols and spaces into one separator, and trimming the ends. The tool also drops letters outside the a-z range after accent removal, especially for non-Latin scripts. It does not shorten slugs or remove common words, and the result might require a manual check if the title is mostly composed of symbols.

Slug Generator: Tips for Best Results

For best results with the Slug Generator, start by ensuring your title is clear and relevant. Use simple language and avoid unnecessary words or symbols. The tool will automatically convert your text to lowercase and remove accents, replacing them with their corresponding letters (e.g., café becomes cafe). Choose either hyphens or underscores as separators based on your preference. Remember, the tool collapses repeated symbols and spaces into a single separator and trims any at the beginning or end of the slug. Since the tool runs entirely in your browser, no text is uploaded or stored, keeping your content private. Keep an eye on the resulting slug; if it's mostly symbols, you may need to manually adjust it for readability.

Slug Generator: Questions & Answers

What does the Slug Generator do?

It converts titles to lowercase slugs with hyphens or underscores, removing accents and extra symbols.

Is my text saved when using the Slug Generator?

No, your text is processed in the browser without uploading or storing it.

Can I use spaces in my title?

Spaces are collapsed into a single separator; avoid them for cleaner results.

Does the Slug Generator handle special characters?

Yes, it removes accents and converts special chars like café to cafe.

Is there a limit to how long my title can be?

There's no strict length limit, but very long titles may look messy after processing.
